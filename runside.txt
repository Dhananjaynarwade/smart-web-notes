What changes after restart is only this: your development servers are stopped. You need to start them again.

Backend:

cd /d "D:\Dhananjay AWS\Django Projects\smart-web-notes\backend"

.venv\Scripts\activate.bat

python manage.py runserver

Frontend in another terminal:

cd /d "D:\Dhananjay AWS\Django Projects\smart-web-notes\frontend"

ng serve

Then open:

http://localhost:4200

and your saved notes should load from Django again.
